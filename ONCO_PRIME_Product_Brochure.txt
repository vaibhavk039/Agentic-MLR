INDICATION
ONCO-PRIME (bevacizumab-alfa) is indicated for the treatment of adult patients
with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (mNSCLC) whose tumors do not have
EGFR or ALK genomic tumor aberrations, as detected by an FDA-approved test,
as first-line therapy in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLINICAL EFFICACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STELLAR-2 Phase III Trial Results

Overall Survival (OS):
  • Median OS: 18.2 months (ONCO-PRIME arm) vs 14.6 months (placebo arm)
  • Hazard Ratio (HR): 0.72 (95% CI: 0.61–0.85)
  • p-value < 0.001

Progression-Free Survival (PFS):
  • Median PFS: 7.8 months vs 5.2 months
  • HR: 0.65 (95% CI: 0.54–0.78)

Overall Response Rate (ORR):
  • 38% in ONCO-PRIME arm vs 19% in placebo arm (p < 0.001)

Study Design:
  N=820 patients randomized 1:1 to ONCO-PRIME 15 mg/kg IV Q3W +
  carboplatin/paclitaxel vs placebo + carboplatin/paclitaxel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Most Common Adverse Reactions (≥ 10%):
  • Hypertension (34%)
  • Fatigue (28%)
  • Epistaxis (22%)
  • Proteinuria (18%)
  • Headache (16%)
  • Nausea (15%)
  • Decreased appetite (12%)

BOXED WARNINGS:
  ■ Gastrointestinal Perforations and Fistulae
  ■ Surgery and Wound Healing Complications
  ■ Hemorrhage

Contraindications:
  Do not administer to patients with uncontrolled hypertension, active bleeding,
  or recent arterial thromboembolic events.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOSING AND ADMINISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Recommended dose: 15 mg/kg IV infusion over 90 minutes (first infusion)
  Subsequent infusions may be administered over 60 minutes if first is tolerated
  Administered every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity
