CARDIO-SHIELD™ (sacubitril/valsartan) 24mg/26mg, 49mg/51mg, 97mg/103mg
Detail Aid — HCP Promotional Material — DRAFT
Submitted for MLR Pre-Screening Review
================================================================================

INDICATION
CARDIO-SHIELD is indicated to reduce the risk of cardiovascular death and
hospitalization for heart failure in adult patients with chronic heart failure
(NYHA Class II-IV) and reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF). CARDIO-SHIELD is
usually administered in conjunction with other heart failure therapies, in place
of an ACE inhibitor or other ARB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MECHANISM OF ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CARDIO-SHIELD contains sacubitril (neprilysin inhibitor) and valsartan
(angiotensin II receptor blocker):

  • Sacubitril is metabolized to LBQ657, which inhibits neprilysin
  • Neprilysin inhibition increases natriuretic peptide levels (ANP, BNP)
  • Results in vasodilation, natriuresis, and reduced cardiac remodeling
  • Valsartan blocks angiotensin II type 1 (AT1) receptors
  • Combined: reduces cardiac workload via dual neurohormonal pathway inhibition

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LANDMARK CLINICAL EVIDENCE: PARADIGM-HF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Primary Endpoint: Composite of CV death or first heart failure hospitalization
  • CARDIO-SHIELD: 21.8%  |  Enalapril: 26.5%
  • Relative Risk Reduction: 20%  |  HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.73–0.87), p<0.001
  • Absolute Risk Reduction: 4.7%  |  NNT: 21 over median 27 months

Cardiovascular Death:
  • 13.3% vs 16.5%  |  HR 0.80, p<0.001

Heart Failure Hospitalization:
  • 12.8% vs 15.6%  |  HR 0.79, p<0.001

All-Cause Mortality:
  • 17.0% vs 19.8%  |  HR 0.84 (95% CI 0.76–0.93), p<0.001

PARADIGM-HF enrolled 8,442 patients with HFrEF (EF ≤ 40%) on stable ACE
inhibitor or ARB therapy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY — MOST COMMON ADVERSE REACTIONS (>5%)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Hypotension             18%
  Hyperkalemia            12%
  Renal Impairment        10%
  Cough                    9%
  Dizziness                7%

WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS:
  ■ BOXED WARNING: Fetal Toxicity — Discontinue when pregnancy is detected
  ■ Hypotension — particularly in patients with low sodium or high-dose diuretics
  ■ Impaired renal function — monitor serum creatinine and potassium
  ■ Hyperkalemia — monitor and dose adjust potassium-sparing agents
  ■ Angioedema — contraindicated in patients with history of ACE-inhibitor
    angioedema; wait 36 hours after stopping ACE inhibitor before starting

CONTRAINDICATIONS:
  • Concomitant use with ACE inhibitors (within 36 hours)
  • History of angioedema with prior ACE inhibitor/ARB therapy
  • Coadministration with aliskiren in patients with diabetes

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Starting dose: 49/51 mg twice daily (may start at 24/26 mg if ACE-inhibitor
  naïve or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m² or moderate hepatic impairment)
  Target dose: 97/103 mg twice daily (double dose every 2–4 weeks as tolerated)
