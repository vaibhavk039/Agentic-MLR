--- DRAFT POST 2 (LinkedIn — Long form) ---

Advancing the Standard of Care in Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Data from the KEYNOTE-PRO trial continue to demonstrate the survival benefit of
IMMUNO-BOOST (pembrolizumab-beta) in patients with previously untreated mNSCLC
whose tumors express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%) with no EGFR/ALK alterations.

Key Data Highlights:
▶ Median OS: 26.3 months with IMMUNO-BOOST vs 13.4 months with chemotherapy
▶ 5-year OS rate: 31.9% vs 16.3%
▶ Durable responses: median DOR 38.9 months
▶ ORR: 44.7% vs 27.9%

IMMUNO-BOOST is indicated as single-agent therapy for adult patients with
unresectable or metastatic NSCLC with PD-L1 expression TPS ≥50% (as determined
by an FDA-approved test), with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumor aberrations, as
first-line treatment.

⚠ IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION
IMMUNO-BOOST can cause severe and fatal immune-mediated adverse reactions
including pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis,
exfoliative dermatitis, myocarditis, and solid organ transplant rejection.

Monitor for signs and symptoms of immune-mediated adverse reactions. Withhold
or permanently discontinue IMMUNO-BOOST based on reaction severity.
